💡 Ethical AI Notice

Remember that artificial intelligence is meant to supplement your intellectual curiosity, not replace it. Use these systems to clarify difficult concepts, verify data references, and test your own understanding through simulation. Always verify crucial data points and ensure your final work accurately reflects your original voice and critical thoughts.
